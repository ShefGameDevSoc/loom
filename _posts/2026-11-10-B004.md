Steel jam is a 26-hour game jam, where teams of up to 4 compete to create a game that fits our theme for the chance to win big prizes.

Whether you're a programmer, artist or designer, whether you're in it to win it or just here for the free food: Steel jam is an unforgettable weekend that you don't want to miss.

We also have a team making social running on Wednesday at 3:00-5:00 in the Diamond workroom 1

Voting for themes will start at 11am on the 14th with the game jam officially beginning at 12. We will then be in the diamond until 9:30 when the building closes and come back at 9am the next day!
